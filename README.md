## About Me

I discovered the idea of code when I was 23 after buying a second-hand laptop to watch movies. I bought that laptop after working as a waiter in a cafe. Till 23, I did every kind of job, from salesman in a cloth shop to working at a civil engineering company. Yeah, it sounds strange, but I had no background in these great things, not even a single person in my surroundings. I started coding at 24, after finally understanding what programming actually is. Honestly, before that, I had no idea about any of this.

I started tinkering, learning, and building, and found out this is a journey of constant suffering. But while doing that, I got the instinct of a builder more than just a coder or programmer. Now I work as a builder, or you can say as a founder. Actually, I see myself as a combination of both.
